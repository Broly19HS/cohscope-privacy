COHScope · Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

COHScope is an independently developed, unofficial statistics companion for Company of Heroes 3. This policy describes how the app handles game information, local storage, network requests, and Android backups.

1. Publisher and contact

App: COHScope
Responsible publisher / data controller: Hasan Saker (individual developer)
Privacy inquiries: broly19hs@gmail.com

2. Information handled by the app

COHScope displays and analyzes publicly available game-related information, which may include player aliases, Relic profile IDs, leaderboard ratings and rankings, competitive match results, participants, maps, factions, and historical match-time ratings. Player aliases and IDs can be personal data if linked to an identifiable person.

When you search for a player, the app handles the player alias that you enter. COHScope does not require you to register a COHScope account.

3. External game-data services

Relic Entertainment: The app makes HTTPS requests to coh3-api.reliclink.com for game data. Depending on the feature, requests include a searched player alias or a Relic profile identifier. This information is used to retrieve player statistics and recent match data.

COH3 Stats: The app incorporates dated open-data snapshots from COH3 Stats and may download leaderboard snapshot files via HTTPS from storage.coh3stats.com. Snapshot downloads use date, faction and game-mode information in their request paths.

As with normal internet requests, these providers may receive technical connection information, including the requesting IP address. COHScope does not control their server-side logging or retention practices.

For source information, see COH3 Stats Open Data.

4. Storage on your device

The app maintains SQLite databases within its Android application storage. They contain historical competitive match records, match-time ratings, player-statistics caches, and leaderboard snapshots. This enables faster access to previously downloaded data and preservation of locally accumulated match history.

The current coverage targets available competitive PvP match information from 1 September 2026 onward. Coverage is dependent on available source data and does not represent any player's guaranteed complete lifetime record.

The release described here does not require an account on a COHScope-operated service, and no public developer-operated match-history backend is configured.

5. Android backups and device transfer

COHScope permits Android to include the match-history and match-time-rating databases in supported operating-system cloud backups and device-to-device transfers. Whether a backup happens depends on device support, backup provider, user settings, and applicable quota limits.

The app's backup allowlist does not include the rebuildable leaderboard and player-statistics caches. Data placed in an Android or Google-managed backup environment is subject to the provider's separate handling, security, retention, and deletion mechanisms. Restoring a backup is not guaranteed.

6. Purposes and security

Game-related information is processed to perform player lookup, retrieve and display statistics and leaderboards, analyze matches, and support local history and caching. The production data-provider requests identified in the app audit use HTTPS. On-device databases benefit from Android's application-storage protections; platform-managed backups use the relevant provider's security mechanisms.

7. Analytics and advertising

COHScope version 1.0.0 does not integrate an advertising SDK or third-party application-analytics SDK. This does not affect data processing that external game-data services may perform when contacted or platform-level diagnostics controlled by Android or Google.

8. Retention and deletion

Locally accumulated game information generally remains in the app's databases until deleted as a result of clearing the application's storage, uninstalling the app, or other device storage operations. There is no in-app COHScope account to delete.

Clearing COHScope application storage can permanently erase match history that is not backed up. Android may restore backed-up information after reinstallation depending on user and device settings. To manage or remove platform backups, use the settings and controls provided by the backup service.

COHScope cannot directly delete records held independently by Relic, COH3 Stats, or Android backup providers.
