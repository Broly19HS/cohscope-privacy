Request data deletion

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This page explains how to ask the COHScope developer to delete feedback you have submitted, and what happens to other app data.

How to request deletion

COHScope does not create user accounts. You do not need an account, password, or access to the Android app to request deletion.

Send an email to broly19hs@gmail.com with the subject COHScope data deletion request.

To help us find the correct feedback report, include any details you have: its report ID (if known), approximate submission date/time, the report category, and a short excerpt of the message. You do not need to send your password, government ID, or other sensitive information. We may ask for additional details only where reasonably necessary to locate the report or prevent unauthorized access to someone else's data.

Start here: Email a COHScope data deletion request. If your email app does not open, copy broly19hs@gmail.com into any email service.

What can be deleted

For a feedback report we can identify, a valid deletion request can result in removal of the report's text, category, optional app version, status, report ID, and submission timestamp from the COHScope-operated Cloudflare D1 feedback database. Where we still hold private CSV exports containing that report, we will also address those copies, subject to any applicable legal retention obligations.

Because feedback reports are anonymous and no account is used, we may be unable to locate or verify an individual report if insufficient information is available.

Retention and limits

Feedback reports in the D1 database are scheduled for automatic deletion after they are older than 90 days, with cleanup configured daily at 04:00 UTC. Technical failures can delay cleanup. CSV exports stored separately by the developer are not automatically deleted by the D1 cleanup task and must be managed separately.

Deletion-request correspondence and limited records necessary to comply with legal obligations or handle legitimate disputes may be retained where applicable law permits or requires it. We will explain material limitations when responding to your request. Technical connection data that Cloudflare processes under its own policies is not stored in the COHScope feedback-report table.

We will respond without undue delay and, when the GDPR applies, generally within one month of receiving the request, subject to permitted extensions.

Data stored on your device or by other services

COHScope stores game-history and cache data locally on your Android device. You can remove this app-managed local data by clearing COHScope's storage or uninstalling the app, but this may permanently erase locally accumulated match history. Android backups or device transfers may retain copies under your device or backup provider's settings.

COHScope cannot delete independent records held by Relic Entertainment, COH3 Stats, Google, or other third-party providers. Contact those providers regarding data they control.
